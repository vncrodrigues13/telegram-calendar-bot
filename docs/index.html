Telegram → Google Calendar Event Bot

A personal, single-user Telegram bot that turns a forwarded event invite into a Google Calendar event — after you approve it.

What it does

You forward an invite to the bot on Telegram — a birthday message, a wedding invitation, a race registration, a screenshot of a flyer. The bot reads it, extracts the title, type, person, place and time, and replies with what it found plus approve, correct and discard buttons.

Nothing reaches your calendar until you tap approve. When you do, the bot creates a single event on your own Google Calendar, keeping the original message in the event description.

Who it is for

This is a personal-use project, run by its owner on their own machine for their own calendar. It is not a hosted service, has no sign-up, and accepts messages from exactly one configured Telegram account — messages from any other account are ignored without a reply.

Google Calendar access

The bot requests one narrow OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events, which allows it to create calendar events. It is used for nothing else. The bot does not read your existing calendar, does not share your data, and does not use it for advertising or to train any model.

See the Privacy Policy for the full detail, including what is stored and how to revoke access at any time.

How it is built

A language model does one narrow job: pulling structured fields out of unstructured text. Approval and event creation are ordinary deterministic code. The project is written in Python and runs locally via long polling — no server, no webhook, no always-on machine.
