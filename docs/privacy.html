Privacy Policy

Telegram → Google Calendar Event Bot

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy explains what the Telegram → Google Calendar Event Bot (the “Bot”) does with data, including data obtained from Google APIs. The Bot is a personal, single-user application operated by an individual for their own Google Calendar. There is no company, no sign-up, and no other users.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the single instance of the Bot operated by its author — the application registered on the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen as “Telegram → Google Calendar Event Bot”, which is the only instance running under the author’s OAuth client credentials.

The Bot’s source code is published at github.com/vncrodrigues13/telegram-calendar-bot. Anyone may obtain a copy and run it, but doing so requires registering their own Google Cloud project and their own OAuth credentials. Copies, forks, and modified versions are therefore separate applications, operated by and under the control of whoever runs them. This policy does not describe or govern them, and questions about such a copy should be directed to the person operating it.

Who operates the Bot

The Bot is operated by an individual for personal use. For any question about privacy or data handling, contact vncrodrigues13@gmail.com.

What the Bot accesses

The Bot handles two kinds of information:

  • Messages you send it on Telegram. The text and images you choose to forward to the Bot, in order to extract event details from them. The Bot only accepts messages from the single Telegram account configured as its owner; messages from any other account are ignored.
  • Google Calendar write access. The Bot requests one OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events. This permits creating and managing calendar events. The Bot uses it solely to create the events you explicitly approve.

The Bot does not request access to your email, contacts, files, profile details, or the contents of your existing calendar.

How the information is used

  • The message you forward is sent to a third-party language model provider (by default Google Gemini) for the single purpose of extracting the event’s title, type, person, place and time.
  • The extracted details are shown back to you for approval. Nothing is written to your calendar unless you explicitly approve it.
  • On approval, one calendar event is created on your own Google Calendar, with the original message text kept in the event description for your reference.

What is stored, and where

The Bot runs on the operator’s own machine. It stores, locally on that machine only:

  • Pending approvals — the extracted event details and original message for an invite awaiting your decision, in a local SQLite database file. These exist only until you approve or discard the invite.
  • Your Google OAuth token — in a local file (token.json) so you do not have to sign in repeatedly.

There is no server, no hosted database, and no analytics. Google user data is not copied to any external storage by the Bot.

Sharing and disclosure

The Bot does not sell, rent, or share your information. The only outbound transfers are the ones required to function:

  • Telegram — necessarily receives the messages you send to the Bot, under Telegram’s Privacy Policy.
  • The configured language model provider (Google Gemini by default; optionally Anthropic or OpenAI) — receives the forwarded message text or image in order to extract event fields, under that provider’s own terms.
  • Google Calendar API — receives the event you approve, in order to create it on your calendar.

Limited Use of Google user data

The Bot’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is:

  • used only to provide the event-creation feature described above;
  • never transferred to others except as required to provide that feature, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law;
  • never used for advertising purposes;
  • never used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models;
  • never read by a human, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymized.

Retention and deletion

Pending approvals are removed once you act on them. Events created on your calendar belong to you and remain there until you delete them in Google Calendar. To erase everything the Bot holds locally, delete its database file and token.json.

Revoking access

You can withdraw the Bot’s Google Calendar access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the Bot from creating any further events.

Children

The Bot is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone other than its single operator.
